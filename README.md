## 1) Understanding the Trent Entry Test

### Official information
- Trent AQ entry-test page states Intermediate Math ABQ entry testing is based on **Ontario Grades 9 and 10 mathematics curriculum**.

### Study recommendation (this package)
- Prepare as if **both Grade 9 and Grade 10 Academic** are major domains.
- Use an **understand -> worked example -> practice -> mixed application** cycle.
- Track errors and quickly revisit prerequisites.

## 6) Pre-Study Diagnostic (36 Questions)

> Covers Grade 9 and Grade 10 Academic. Use it to set priorities, not to predict pass/fail.

1. Simplify \(5x-2(3x-4)\).  
2. Solve \(2x+9=3x-5\).

5. Is \(y=2x^2-3x+1\) linear or non-linear? Why?  
6. Make a table for \(y=-x+4\), x=-2..3.

8. Rearrange \(V=IR\) for R.  
9. Plot and identify slope of \(y=-\frac{3}{2}x+1\).  
10. Find midpoint of A(-4,6), B(8,-2).  
11. Distance between (2,1) and (8,10).

13. Determine equation of line through (2,-1) and (6,7).  
14. Expand \((x-5)^2\).  
15. Factor \(x^2-7x+12\).  
16. Solve \(x^2-9=0\).  
17. Find zeros of \(y=x^2+2x-8\).  
18. State axis of symmetry for \(y=2x^2-8x+1\).  
19. Vertex of \(y=(x-3)^2-5\).  
20. Does \(y=-2(x+1)^2+7\) open up or down?  
21. Solve right triangle: hypotenuse 13, one leg 5.  
22. Right triangle: opposite=7, adjacent=24; find angle.  
23. Use cosine law: sides 8,10 included angle 60°, find third side.  
24. Use sine law: \(a=9\), A=30°, B=45°; find b.  
25. Surface area of cylinder r=3, h=10.  
26. Volume of triangular prism (base area 18, length 7).  
27. Identify one possible bias in an online poll.  
28. From data set 3,5,7,7,9 compute mean and median.  
29. Theoretical probability of drawing red from 5 red, 7 blue cards.  
30. Experimental probability: 18 heads in 40 flips.  
31. Compare simple vs compound interest idea (conceptual).  
32. A laptop depreciates 12% yearly from $1200; value after 1 year.  
33. Budget changes: income drops by 8%; list 2 reasonable adjustments.  
34. Determine whether relation represented by table has constant second difference (given sequence 3,8,15,24).  
35. Explain why equivalent algebraic forms can aid graphing.  
36. Solve multi-step: rectangle perimeter 50, length is 3 more than width.

### Diagnostic skill interpretation
- **0–18:** rebuild fluency + Grade 9 first.
- **19–28:** mixed readiness; prioritize weak strands from mapping.
- **29–36:** focus on Grade 10 integration, speed, and error reduction.

## 8) Lesson Set — Grade 9 (B–F)

> **Priority labels:** CORE / IMPORTANT / SUPPORTING (study recommendation only)

### G9-L1 Number Sets and Number-System Development (B1.1, B1.2, B1.3) — IMPORTANT
**Curriculum connection:** Grade 9, Strand B, B1.1/B1.2/B1.3  
**Why this matters:** Supports precision with rational/irrational reasoning and later algebra/trig definitions.  
**Prerequisites:** Integers, fractions, decimals, basic set notation.  
**Core concept:** Real-number system structure, density, and infinite sets.  
**Key vocabulary:** natural, integer, rational, irrational, real, density, limit.  
**Key rules/formulas:** Rational = ratio of integers; irrationals non-terminating non-repeating decimals.  
**Worked example:** Classify \(\sqrt{49},\sqrt{2},-3.75,\frac{11}{3}\) by subsets; justify membership.  
**Test-level example:** Explain why between any two rationals there are infinitely many rationals.  
**Common mistakes:** Treating repeating decimals as irrational; assuming “next” real number exists.  
**Practice (8):** classify numbers; construct examples; density proof sketch; interval questions.

### G9-L2 Exponents and Scientific Notation (B2.1, B2.2) — CORE
- Apply exponent laws, negative exponents, standard/scientific notation conversion, and operation fluency.
- Worked example: simplify \((3x^2y^{-1})(2x^{-5}y^3)\).
- Test-level example: population model in scientific notation with unit scaling.
- Practice (8): mixed simplification, notation conversion, contextual quantity estimation.

### G9-L3 Expressions in Context (C1.1, C1.2) — CORE
- Compare equivalent forms, evaluate substituted expressions, choose efficient form.
- Worked example: compare \(2(x+3)+x\) and \(3x+6\) algebraically and numerically.
- Practice (8): write expressions from context; evaluate; compare forms.

### G9-L4 Formula Rearrangement + Linear Equations (C1.3, C1.4) — CORE
- Solve/rearrange first-degree equations; verify by substitution.
- Worked example: from \(P=2l+2w\), solve for \(w\); then compute for given values.
- Test-level example: multi-step word problem requiring equation setup.
- Practice (10).

### G9-L5 Coding and Computational Representations (C2.1, C2.2) — SUPPORTING
- Pseudocode/blocks/text logic to represent linear/non-linear patterns.
- Worked example: edit loop code generating \(y=2x+1\) to \(y=x^2-1\).
- Practice (6).

### G9-L6 Relations: Tables, Graphs, Equations, Conclusions (C3.1–C3.3) — CORE
- Identify linear vs non-linear relations and use representations to predict/interpolate.
- Worked example: same relation represented by table and equation; extract rate and intercept.
- Test-level example: choose best model and justify with data pattern.
- Practice (10).

### G9-L7 Data Literacy and Displays (D1.1, D1.2) — IMPORTANT
- Critique graphs for bias/scales; organize one/two-variable data displays.
- Worked example: detect misleading vertical scale and rewrite conclusion.
- Practice (8).

### G9-L8 Statistical Investigation and Ethics (D1.3) — SUPPORTING
- Sampling method choice, survey bias, ethics of data collection.
- Worked example: redesign biased survey question.
- Practice (6).

### G9-L9 Probability and Argument (D2.1, D2.2) — IMPORTANT
- Theoretical vs experimental probability; reasonableness and argument quality.
- Worked example: compare simulated vs theoretical results for two-dice sum.
- Test-level example: critique probability claim in media context.
- Practice (8).

### G9-L10 Geometry + Measurement (E1.1, E1.2) — CORE
- 2D/3D properties and perimeter/area/SA/volume in composite contexts.
- Worked example: surface area of composite prism/cylinder object.
- Practice (10).

### G9-L11 Transformations and Coordinate Geometry (E2.1, E2.2) — CORE
- Transformations and line-equation coordinate tasks.
- Worked example: reflect triangle in y-axis then translate; give new coordinates.
- Test-level example: determine equation of transformed line.
- Practice (8).

### G9-L12 Financial Contexts and Change Graphs (F1.1, F1.2) — IMPORTANT
- Appreciation/depreciation and financial interpretation from graphs.
- Worked example: depreciation table to equation estimate.
- Practice (8).

### G9-L13 Interest, Borrowing, Budget Revision (F1.3, F1.4) — CORE
- Compare borrowing options; adjust budget under constraints.
- Worked example: compare loan scenarios with differing rates/terms/down payments.
- Test-level example: revise budget after income drop + unavoidable expense increase.
- Practice (10).

## 14) Rapid Review Sheet (Grade 9 + Grade 10)

### Algebra
- Exponent laws: \(a^ma^n=a^{m+n}\), \(a^{-n}=1/a^n\), \((ab)^n=a^nb^n\).
- Linear form: \(y=mx+b\), slope \(m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\).
- Quadratic forms: standard \(ax^2+bx+c\), vertex \(a(x-h)^2+k\), factored \(a(x-r_1)(x-r_2)\).

### Analytic geometry
- Midpoint \(\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)\), distance \(\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}\).
- Parallel lines: equal slopes; perpendicular: negative reciprocal slopes.

### Geometry/Measurement
- Triangle area \(A=\frac12 bh\), circle area \(\pi r^2\), cylinder volume \(\pi r^2h\).

### Trigonometry
- Right triangle: \(\sin\theta=\frac{opp}{hyp}\), \(\cos\theta=\frac{adj}{hyp}\), \(\tan\theta=\frac{opp}{adj}\).
- Sine law: \(\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}\).
- Cosine law: \(c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C\).

### Data/Probability
- Mean \(\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x}{n}\), median middle value, probability \(P(E)=\frac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}}\).

### Finance
- Percent change: new = old\((1\pm r)\).  
- Simple interest: \(I=Prt\).  
- Compound growth/decay: \(A=P(1+r)^t\) (as applicable in course contexts).
